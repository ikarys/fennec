# Welcome to fennec

Your coding agent proposes, you decide. Nothing reaches your branch without your click.

fennec runs on your machine, in your browser. You describe a change; an agent reads your code,
proposes a plan, builds it in a separate copy, checks it, then waits for you. You read what it
checked and choose: **Integrate**, or discard.

> **Tip:** new here? Follow [Install fennec](install.md): ten minutes from install to a first
> change you approved.
